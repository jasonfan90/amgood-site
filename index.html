Two people connect. Each sees one line about the other, once a day: whether their phone showed any activity on days that are already over.

  • Never anything about today.

Fair in both directions

There are no parents' accounts and kids' accounts. Both people get the same app and see the same thing about each other. Each of you chooses what the other sees, and each can see what the other chose. The app shows you, word for word, what others see about you.

Nothing to do

No check-ins, no buttons to remember. AmGood works out yes or no on your phone from your step count, and sends only the answer. Put the widget on your Home Screen or Lock Screen and one glance tells you.

Not an emergency service

AmGood tells you that a day which is already over was fine. It can be a day or two behind by design. It does not detect emergencies and must not be relied on for anyone's safety.
